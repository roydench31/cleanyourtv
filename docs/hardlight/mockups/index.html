How to use

1 to 6 switch mockups; the arrow keys move focus like the D-pad.

A · The stock launcher today, cinematic shelf

  • Only config.json and wallpaper.jpg; no code.
  • The shelf lays the focused app's artwork over the whole screen at 50%, so white banners wash the wallpaper out.

B · The stock launcher today, classic dock

  • The same wallpaper with cinematic_home: false: the dock's top edge sits on the emitter line.

C · Hard Light: the rail layout

  • One row of tiles standing on a horizon line, the focused one raised with its reflection and an under-glow.
  • A compact top rail, the app name in a display face, and control hints. See GUIDE.md.

D · Mono Dark

  • Pure black, white type, Outfit only; no wallpaper, glow or reflection.
  • Every banner greyscale and dimmed: the focused app is the only colour on screen.
  • Focus is a 2 px outline; the counter says where you are in the row.

E · Mono Light

  • The same idea on white: near-black ink, light grey hairlines.
  • Needs the fork's light mode (surface_mode: light): pills, focus and settings inverted.

F · Ocean

  • A wave photo, graded to deep sea on the left and at the bottom so the name, hints and tiles read over it.
  • Great Vibes for the greeting and the app name; Outfit for everything else.
  • The focused tile's shadow takes its logo's colour, computed from the banner the way the launcher's vividColor does.
  • The horizon is a shoreline: a gentle wave instead of a straight line.
